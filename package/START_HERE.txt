CO2 BACKFILL PREDICTOR - HOW TO START
=====================================

The app runs on your own computer, in your web browser. Nothing is sent to the internet,
and your data stays on your computer.

ONE-TIME SETUP (about 10 minutes)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere easy to find, for example the Desktop.
2. If Python is not installed yet: install Python 3.12 from https://www.python.org/downloads/
   On Windows, tick "Add python.exe to PATH" on the first screen of the installer.
3. Double-click the start file:
     Windows: "Start app (Windows).bat"
     Mac:     "Start app (Mac).command"
              (if the Mac says it cannot open it: right-click the file, choose Open, then Open again)
   The first time, it installs what it needs (5-10 minutes, needs internet).

EVERY TIME AFTER THAT
Double-click the same start file. The app opens in your browser at http://localhost:8501.
The first page takes about 1 minute while the models train. Keep the black window open
while you use the app; close it to stop the app.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Set the mix on the left (CO2 time, cement : tailings ratio, stirring speed, CO2 flow, curing age).
  Predict ............ predicted porosity with a 95% range, and the best CO2 injection time.
  Compare models ..... the 8 machine-learning models side by side.
  Check new lab data . type or upload new lab results and see how close the model was.
  Retrain (optional) . add your new results to the model and see whether it improves.
                       The original data file is never changed.
  Next tests ......... the CO2 times we suggest testing next: 6, 7, 9, 11, 12 and 13 min.
  About and limits ... what the data and model are, and their limits.
Every table has a "Download (CSV)" button.

SENDING NEW LAB RESULTS
In "Check new lab data", click "Download a blank template (CSV)", fill in the last column
(porosity_pct) in Excel, save as CSV, and upload it. Please also send the file to Zayn.

PLEASE NOTE
These are preliminary results. The training data were derived from the published JCP 2025
results, not raw lab data, and the 95% ranges are too narrow (they held 63-77% of test
points, not 95%).

Questions or problems: contact Zayn.
